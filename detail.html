Elevating Operations: SFI Global's Commitment to Exceptional Forklift and Machinery Services

At SFI Global SDN. BHD., we take pride in revolutionizing industrial and logistics operations across Malaysia. Our machinery fleet—headlined by Almiza material handling solutions, high-reach electric reach trucks, and heavy-duty diesel counterbalance units—is backed by end-to-end preventative maintenance and licensed operators.

Discover how our equipment rental and acquisition services go beyond conventional leasing. From DOSH-certified machinery safety inspections to comprehensive operator safety programs, we ensure precision, maximum workplace safety, and peak fuel or battery efficiency in every shift.

In addition to machinery, SFI Global integrates specialized industrial services including temperature-controlled cold storage warehousing, certified general and skilled manpower supply, and green industrial scrap and waste handling to give your enterprise a seamless single-source partner.
