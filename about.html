Corporate Heritage

Welcome To SFI Global SDN.BHD

SFI Global with a proud history of more than fifteen years has witnessed and been part of the spectacular evolution of Malaysia into a modern developed nation. Headquartered in Shah Alam, SFI Global operates nationwide.

All of our manpower outsourcing, warehousing, equipment rentals, and IT solutions are bespoke to each client's operational requirements. We ensure precision supply chain integration and high workforce productivity — which is why our client retention rate exceeds 99%.

To become Malaysia's premier business services and manpower partner by providing reliable opportunities for enterprises to collaborate with highly skilled, motivated professionals.

Our Mission
