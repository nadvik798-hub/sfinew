Welcome To SFI Global

SFI Global with a proud history of more than fifteen years has the privilege to have witnessed and been part of the spectacular evolution of Malaysia into a modern developed nation with its headquarters in Shah Alam, SFI Global operates all over Malaysia.

All of our warehousing services are bespoke to each individual customer to ensure they get the best solution for their supply chain – that's just one of the reasons why we have so many long-standing customers.
